## Scope of computation

Both simulation suites were computed for this release rather than repackaged;
each records `historical_results_consumed: false`, enforced by
`scripts/verify_frozen_results.py`. Runtimes on 24 logical cores with 12
workers: 3 h 13 min (all-Poisson), 3 h 38 min (mixed-family).

The ten NBA optimizations were not rerun for this release; their frozen outputs
were verified. One control refit was performed: season 2015-16 under the pinned
NBA environment reproduced the committed result with a score difference of 0
and identical BICs at all five nodes. Under an unpinned NumPy the same refit
differed by 2.3e-05 and changed one node's selected exogenous family, which is
why `scripts/check_environment.py` gates the refit entry points.

## Limitations

Stated in `docs/UNRESOLVED_PROVENANCE.md`: the negative-binomial convolution
defect and which core each line uses, the unlicensed external baselines, and
the non-redistributable upstream NBA data.
