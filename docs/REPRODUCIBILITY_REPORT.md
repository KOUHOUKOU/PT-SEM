## Refitting

The two simulation suites were recomputed for this release rather than
repackaged: both record `historical_results_consumed: false`, and
`verify_frozen_results.py` enforces it. Recorded runtimes on 24 logical cores
with 12 workers were 3 h 13 min (all-Poisson) and 3 h 38 min (mixed-family).

The ten NBA optimizations were not rerun for this release; their frozen outputs
were verified instead. One control refit was performed: season 2015-16 under the
pinned NBA environment reproduced the committed result exactly, with a score
difference of 0 and identical BICs at all five nodes. The same refit under an
unpinned NumPy differed by 2.3e-05 and flipped one node's selected family, which
is why the environment check is enforced rather than advised.

## Scope of what is shipped

`outputs/` contains the manuscript's objects and nothing else. Per-replication
tables, per-family local scores, optimizer diagnostics and run manifests are
kept where the programs write them and are not presented as results. Artifacts
with no correspondence in the current manuscript were removed rather than
retained.

## Known limitations

The NB convolution defect, the retained historical core on the NBA line, the
unlicensed external baselines, the non-redistributable upstream NBA data, and
the lost intermediates of the superseded server run are each stated in
`docs/UNRESOLVED_PROVENANCE.md`.
